KNOW YOUR MODEL

An experiment, with its assumptions in view.

Geometry drives the calculation: each triangle contributes a windward pressure force and a small tangential skin-friction force. Rotating or replacing your model changes the predicted forces. The pressure colors show the local modeled pressure coefficient.

How the numbers are calculated

q = ½ρV² · Cp = 2 max(0, −n·u)² · F = Σ(−q Cp n A + friction). The friction coefficient is fixed at 0.006. Cd and Cl use the unrotated X–Z bounding rectangle as the reference area. Reynolds number uses viscosity 1.81 × 10⁻⁵ Pa·s; Mach uses 343 m/s.

This modified Newtonian-style approximation is illustrative at the available low speeds. It does not resolve circulation, suction, stall, separation, turbulence, wake interactions or shielding between surfaces. It cannot predict accurate wing performance and is not CFD. Use closed, consistently outward-wound meshes; concave or overlapping parts may be overcounted.
